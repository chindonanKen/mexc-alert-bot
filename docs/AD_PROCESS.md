# AD process (locked rules book)

Ticker-free. Chart / path / size / fail / exit only.

This is the **process book** for AD Desk. It is **not** `docs/AD_AGENT_PLAN.md` (autonomy roadmap). It is **not** a mixed workspace rules file with names, dollars, or live plays. Those stay out.

---

## Chart

- Visual, not a global formula. Each chart has its own drop length, bounce quality, red count, and fail habit **on the working timeframe**.
- History of **this** chart in **this** price range decides AD, entry, size, and skip. Another range or another timeframe does not import its story.
- If this range has no history yet (new high / new low), wait for history to form.
- Prefer the AD length that historically gave many reliable bounces with manageable downside — not the absolute lowest wick.
- Re-measure when price enters a significantly different range.
- Slow-forming ADs (much longer than prior ADs) = less panic → higher risk. Allow more time, or sit out.

## Path

- Work one structure timeframe. A faster TF may time the same path; it does not replace it.
- Panic path: sharp, fast dump, market-wide selling, volume into the low, clear AD history.
- Sit-out path: grind, isolated dump, dry volume, first or second red at the AD unless the board is in panic, news that kills the name.
- Plan the levels. Alarms wait. Engage only when the path fires. Do not stare the path into existence.
- Scale in on the path. Scale out on a meaningful bounce. Do not all-in on hope.

## Size

- Exponential layers into weakness: small first, larger deeper. Default 5–10 layers.
- Keep powder for extensions past the first AD.
- Few history matches → lean / one layer. Many matches to past winners on this chart → standard or press.
- Near a major base or a risky setup: shrink early size, widen spacing, or no-trade.
- Size is relative weights on the layer ladder — not a dollar claim.

## Fail

- Failed AD: price tags the AD zone and there is **no meaningful bounce** in the time this chart usually needs → exit to preserve capital. Do not marry the thesis.
- Last at or under AD bottom is spent. Do not keep pressing a spent path.
- News flatten (delist / scam / hack / closure) kills the path.
- Charts with repeated catastrophic failed ADs → avoid or tiny size.
- Isolated collapse / pump-and-dump is wipeout territory. No-trade must be clean — do not soften it with a full “if you trade anyway” plan.

## Exit

- Layer exits the same way you layered entries. Do not dump the whole book on the first green unless defensive mode is on.
- A valid bounce must look significant next to the smaller rallies already on this chart. Use this chart’s bounce history (how long / how far).
- Extended drop: defensive — take the bounce that gives small loss / flat / small win.
- Broad market panic: do not panic-exit early; let the event complete, then take the real bounce.
- Sideways too long in the AD zone → exit to preserve capital.
- Pride holds a wrong thesis. Greed skips the exit ladder. Process fail is still a fail when the number is green.

---

Open on the book is **exchange residual** (futures open positions / spot balances). A buy fill is not an open. Telegram does not ping position-opened from a fill.
